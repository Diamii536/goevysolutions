BEFORE WE TALK

A few useful answers.

Do I need a detailed brief?+

No. Start with what is happening, who it affects, and what you would like to change. We can explore the detail together.

Can you help with a single process?+

Yes. An engagement can focus on a particular workflow or a broader operational challenge. Scope is agreed around your priorities.

Do you only work with particular software?+

Our starting point is the business need. We assess the systems you already use and identify suitable changes in that context.

What happens after the first conversation?+

If there is a good fit, we agree the next steps, scope, responsibilities, and expected outputs before work begins.
