How this website handles the information you choose to share.

Information you provide

The enquiry form prepares an email in your own email application. It does not send the form details to a website database. You decide whether to send the prepared email to admin@goevysolutions.org.

How enquiry information is used

When you email Goevy, your name, contact details, organisation, and message are used to understand and respond to your enquiry. Please avoid including confidential information or sensitive personal details in an initial message.

Website storage and external links

This site does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not store form details in your browser. Links to social platforms open services with their own privacy policies. If the site is hosted, the hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses as part of serving the site.

Questions about your information

For questions about information shared with Goevy, or to request its correction or deletion, contact admin@goevysolutions.org.
