CASE STUDIES

Understand the challenge.

Client stories will be published here when approved for release. In the meantime, these illustrative examples show how an engagement could be structured.

Illustrative engagement examples — not client projects or achieved results.

EXAMPLE 01

A manufacturing workflow

The challenge

Work waits between production stages, and responsibility for resolving delays is unclear.

A possible approach

Map handovers, clarify ownership, test a revised workflow, and introduce a regular performance review.

What we would measure

Cycle time, rework, throughput, and time spent waiting.

EXAMPLE 02

A growing service business

The challenge

Client onboarding depends on individual knowledge and information is collected more than once.

A possible approach

Define a consistent intake process, connect the information needed by each team, and document the handover.

What we would measure

Onboarding time, missing information, repeated work, and customer feedback.

EXAMPLE 03

A disconnected reporting process

The challenge

Leaders receive multiple reports but struggle to identify which action to take.

A possible approach

Agree the decisions to support, define a focused KPI framework, and establish data ownership and review routines.

What we would measure

Data completeness, reporting effort, decision turnaround, and action follow-through.
