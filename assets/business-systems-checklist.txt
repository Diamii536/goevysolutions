GOEVY SOLUTIONS
BUSINESS SYSTEMS READINESS CHECKLIST
Better systems. Better decisions. Better ways of working.

For each question, mark: Clear / Needs attention / Not sure.

PEOPLE
1. Does every important workflow have a clear owner?
2. Can someone follow the process without relying on one person's memory?

PROCESSES
3. Are the inputs, handovers, and completion criteria documented?
4. Do you know where work waits, repeats, or regularly fails?
5. Is there a clear route for handling exceptions?

TECHNOLOGY
6. Do your systems support the way teams need to work?
7. Is information entered once and available where it is needed?

DATA & DECISIONS
8. Are your key measures defined consistently, with an owner and data source?
9. Does reporting lead to a decision, an action, and a follow-up?

CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
10. Does the team review what is working and agree the next improvement?

YOUR NEXT STEP
Choose one priority process. Write down the challenge, its owner, the
outcome you want, and one measure that would show progress.

Discuss your priorities: admin@goevysolutions.org
This checklist is a discussion aid, not a scored assessment.
